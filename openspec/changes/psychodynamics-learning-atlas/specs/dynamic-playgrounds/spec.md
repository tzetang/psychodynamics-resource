## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Defense Mechanism Switchboard and Triangle of Conflict Playground
The system SHALL provide an interactive Defense Mechanism Switchboard grounded in Malan's Triangle of Conflict (Impulse/Feeling ↔ Anxiety ↔ Defense), Anna Freud's Idea-vs-Affect model, and Vaillant/Perry's Defense Mechanism Rating Scale (DMRS Levels 1–7).

#### Scenario: Animating Idea and Affect routing through a selected defense
- **WHEN** the user selects an unconscious trigger/impulse and a defense mechanism (e.g., Repression, Isolation of Affect, Intellectualization, Reaction Formation, Projection, Splitting, Humor, or Sublimation) and clicks Play or steps through the animation
- **THEN** the playground visually animates the raw Impulse (Idea + Affect particles) hitting Signal Anxiety, passing through the Defense filter, and showing exactly what happens to the **Idea (representation)** versus the **Affect (emotional charge)** to produce the conscious output, alongside its DMRS maturity level and theoretical/empirical notes.

#### Scenario: Adjusting Ego Capacity / Stress slider
- **WHEN** the user adjusts the Stress / Ego Capacity slider in the Defense Switchboard
- **THEN** the playground highlights which DMRS levels of defense (Mature, Neurotic, Immature/Borderline) become most likely to activate under that stress threshold.

### Requirement: Internal Object Theater Playground (Splitting vs. Integration)
The system SHALL provide an interactive Object Relations playground grounded in Melanie Klein's Paranoid-Schizoid vs. Depressive positions, Otto Kernberg's Self-Object-Affect dyads, and Peter Fonagy's Mentalization / Reflective Functioning.

#### Scenario: Simulating relational triggers under Split vs. Integrated states
- **WHEN** the user selects a relational trigger (e.g., "Friend cancels plans", "Partner gives mild criticism", "Mentor praises work") and drags the Integration / Mentalization slider between Paranoid-Schizoid (Split) and Depressive (Integrated) positions
- **THEN** the visual stage animates how the internal Self-Representation and Object-Representation either oscillate wildly across a rigid splitting barrier (All-Good idealized vs. All-Bad persecutory dyads) or integrate into a cohesive, ambivalent whole capable of object constancy.

### Requirement: Interpersonal Loop and CCRT Simulator Playground
The system SHALL provide an interactive two-person relational simulator grounded in Luborsky's Core Conflictual Relationship Theme (CCRT: Wish → Response from Other → Response of Self), Transference, and Projective Identification enactments.

#### Scenario: Stepping through a self-fulfilling interpersonal loop vs. therapeutic interruption
- **WHEN** the user selects a relational template/CCRT pattern and steps through the animated cycle between Person A and Person B
- **THEN** the simulator animates the 4-stage loop (Unconscious Wish & Fear → Defensive/Provocative Signal → Pulled Response from Other → Confirming Response of Self) and allows toggling between "Enactment Loop Continues" and "Corrective / Mentalizing Interruption" to see how the cycle resolves.
